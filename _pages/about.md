# About Me

I am a PhD student in Medical Imaging with research interests in deep learning, computer vision, and artificial intelligence for medical image analysis.

My research focuses on developing and evaluating reliable deep-learning methods for medical imaging, with particular interest in object detection, image classification, model evaluation, and understanding whether AI systems are learning clinically meaningful image features.

A major part of my work involves investigating how models make predictions, how accurately they localize abnormalities, and how different factors such as preprocessing, model architecture, and evaluation strategies influence model performance and reliability.

## Research Interests

- Medical Image Analysis
- Deep Learning
- Computer Vision
- Object Detection
- Medical Image Classification
- Image Segmentation
- Explainable Artificial Intelligence
- Reliable and Trustworthy Medical AI
- Model Evaluation and Error Analysis
- Reproducible Machine Learning

## Current Research

My current PhD research explores deep-learning methods for medical image analysis, with a particular focus on the relationship between prediction performance and localization accuracy.

I am interested in understanding whether models are making decisions based on clinically meaningful regions of medical images rather than relying on unrelated or spurious features. This includes investigating false positives, false negatives, localization errors, model confidence, and the limitations of conventional evaluation metrics.

## UniDetect

I am also developing **UniDetect**, a modular research framework for object-detection experiments.

UniDetect is designed to support systematic and reproducible experimentation with different model architectures, preprocessing strategies, training configurations, and evaluation methods. The framework is being developed to make it easier to compare object-detection experiments and to analyze model behavior in medical imaging applications.

## Research Goal

My broader research goal is to contribute toward the development of medical AI systems that are not only accurate, but also reliable, interpretable, and clinically meaningful.
