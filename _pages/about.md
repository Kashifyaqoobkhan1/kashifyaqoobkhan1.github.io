I am a PhD researcher specializing in Medical Image Analysis, with research interests in Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence for healthcare applications.

My research focuses on the development and systematic evaluation of deep learning methods for medical image understanding, including object detection, image classification, and segmentation. I am particularly interested in discriminative feature learning, model generalization, interpretability, and the reliability of AI-based predictions. My work also investigates how model architectures, preprocessing techniques, and evaluation strategies influence performance and the ability of AI systems to identify clinically relevant image features.

## Research Interests

- Medical Image Analysis
- Deep Learning and Computer Vision
- Medical Image Classification, Detection, and Segmentation
- Discriminative and Representation Learning
- Explainable and Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence
- Model Robustness and Generalization
- Model Evaluation, Error Analysis, and Reproducibility

## Current Research

My current research focuses on developing and evaluating deep learning approaches for medical image analysis, with applications in computer-aided diagnosis and abnormality detection.

I am particularly interested in understanding the performance, robustness, and generalization of artificial intelligence models in medical imaging. My work involves investigating computational approaches for image understanding, model evaluation, and the development of reliable and reproducible deep learning methodologies.

My broader objective is to contribute to advancements in artificial intelligence for medical imaging through systematic experimentation and rigorous evaluation.

## UniDetect

I am developing **UniDetect**, a research-oriented framework designed to support organized, systematic, and reproducible experiments in object detection.

The project aims to facilitate experimental management, model evaluation, and comparative analysis across computer vision research applications.

## Research Goal

My broader research goal is to contribute toward the development of medical AI systems that are not only accurate, but also reliable, interpretable, and clinically meaningful.
