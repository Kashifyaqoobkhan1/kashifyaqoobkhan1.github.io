# Research

I am a PhD student in Medical Imaging with research interests in deep learning, computer vision, and artificial intelligence for medical image analysis.

My research focuses on developing and evaluating reliable deep-learning methods for medical imaging, with particular interest in object detection, image classification, localization, model evaluation, and understanding whether AI systems are learning clinically meaningful image features.

## Research Interests

- Medical Image Analysis
- Deep Learning
- Computer Vision
- Object Detection
- Medical Image Classification
- Image Segmentation
- Explainable Artificial Intelligence
- Reliable and Trustworthy Medical AI
- Model Evaluation
- Error Analysis
- Reproducible Machine Learning

## Current Research

My current PhD research investigates the relationship between predictive performance and localization accuracy in medical imaging.

I am particularly interested in understanding whether deep-learning models make predictions based on clinically meaningful regions or whether they rely on unrelated or spurious image features.

This includes the analysis of:

- False positives
- False negatives
- Localization errors
- Model confidence
- Preprocessing effects
- Model architecture
- Evaluation metrics

## Research Software

I am also developing **UniDetect**, a modular research framework for reproducible object-detection experiments.

UniDetect is designed to support systematic comparison of model architectures, preprocessing strategies, training configurations, and evaluation methods in medical imaging research.
